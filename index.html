HGV QA SUCCESS MAP

Solve the member’s real problem and the QA behaviours follow.
Phrases are adaptable examples, not scripts. Use only what fits the call.
1
LISTEN & DISCOVER
Find the real problem, not the first symptom. Learn their goal, worries and limits.
“Tell me more about what’s happening.”
“What matters most to you about the outcome?”
QA: Discovery · Rapport · Flow
▶
2
CLARIFY & CONFIRM
Restate their need and the why. Confirm, never assume.
“So you’re looking to [X] because [Y]. Have I got that right?”
“Have I missed anything that affects your options?”
QA: Understood Needs · Flow · Tone
▶
3
SET EXPECTATIONS
Say what you’ll check and why. Keep control; no silent gaps.
“Here’s what I’ll check first, then we’ll go through your options.”
“I’m reviewing [detail] now. I’ll keep you updated.”
QA: Flow · Tone · Sharing Knowledge · FIR
▶
4
SOLVE & OWN IT
Find the root cause, research accurately, check related concerns, explain why.
“I’ve checked [X], and here’s what I found.”
“Here’s what I can do, and why it’s the right next step.”
QA: FIR · Compensation · Sharing · Tone
▶
5
ADD RELEVANT VALUE
Only what truly fits: benefits, deadlines, options, online tools. Never force it.
“Based on your plans, [X] could help because [goal].”
“Would you like me to show you where to find that online?”
QA: Salesmanship · Self-service · Sharing · Appreciation
▶
6
CONFIRM & CLOSE
Recap, give next steps and timeframes, check understanding, offer more help.
“To recap, we’ve done [X]. Next is [Y], within [timeframe].”
“What else can I clarify before we finish?”
QA: FIR · Understood Needs · Tone · Flow
⇄
1. Conversational Flow
Guide problem → resolution. Transitions, summaries, no dead air.
“Let’s go step by step, and I’ll keep you updated.”
☺
2. Servicing Tone
Warm, confident, positive. “I will” over “I can”; solutions before limits.
“I understand why that matters. Let’s look at the options together.”
♥
3. Owner Appreciation
Personal thanks, acknowledge tenure, give an assurance of help.
“Thank you for being part of our HGV family for [X] years.”
☆
4. Rapport Building
Empathy in their words, use their name, find common ground.
“That sounds like an important trip. Let’s make it work.”
?
5. Discovery Questions
Open and follow-up questions that uncover the “why”.
“What would be an ideal outcome for you today?”
✓
6. Understood Needs
Repeat back, confirm, tailor your response to their cues.
“Let’s review what we have so far. Please correct anything I’ve missed.”
⚖
7. Compensation
Check guidelines and history, explain the rationale, say if it’s one-time.
“After reviewing your account, I can submit a one-time courtesy exception.”
⚑
8. First Interaction Resolution
Answer ALL concerns, check for related issues, give next steps and timeline.
“Before I let you go, let me check your account for deadlines or balances.”
★
9. Salesmanship – Enhancement of Product & Ownership
Tie options, points and deadlines to their goals. Never force.
“I see [X] Points on your account. Are you aware of all the ways to use them?”
⌂
10. Self-servicing Education
Mention the website, ask familiarity, give navigation steps.
“Have you used our site before? I can walk you through it now.”
i
11. Sharing Knowledge
Broader context first, then tailor. Explain why, timelines, next steps.
“What I’m seeing is [X]. It should take [timeframe] to complete.”
HOW SCORING LEVELS DIFFER
MEETS: accurate, answers everything, “good but not great”.
EXCEEDS (+1): finds the root cause, anticipates follow-ups, goes the extra mile.
AVOID: jumping to solutions, “can’t” first, reading a script.
